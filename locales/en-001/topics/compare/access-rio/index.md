# Access Rio

A record for mental-health, community and child-health providers.

A note from public sources, read on 4 November 2026. Nothing here was verified by using the product. Vendor claims are claims. This is not a product review, and "not found" means only that the sources read did not say.

## What it is

Access Rio, previously Servelec Rio, is sold by The Access Group. The vendor says it is used by almost half of the mental-health trusts in England, which was not independently checked.

## Care settings

Mental health is its core market, with the Mental Health Act as a design driver. Community and child health are in scope. Primary care is not its market.

## Comparison

| Area | Access Rio | This project today |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Community, mental-health and child-health record | Scaffold: identity, consent, allergies, observations, referrals, audit |
| Interoperability | Integration Hub (vendor) | FHIR R4 and R5 reads for three resources; HL7 v2 ADT inbound |
| Mobile and patient access | Field recording with sync (vendor); Mood Diary app | Mobile layout and patient portal not built |

## Implications for this project

Legal status should be a dated record with its authority. Discharge from one team should not close the pathway. A single record with several profiles is close to this project’s plan.
