# Privacy

What this site keeps in your browser, why, and what it does not keep.

## What is kept in your browser

This site keeps two settings in your browser's local storage, and only after you choose them in the header:

| Key | What it holds | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `epr-theme` | The colour theme you chose, or `system` to follow your device's light or dark setting | `dark` |
| `epr-text-size` | The text size you chose | `large` |

Each value is one word from a short list. It names a setting, not a person: it holds no name, account, address, identifier or record of the pages you read. A first visit stores nothing until you change a setting.

## Why

So that the theme and text size you chose stay the same on the next page and on your next visit, and the page is drawn in them before it appears.

## What is not kept

- **No cookies.** The theme picker can keep a choice in a cookie or in local storage. This site has no server that could read a cookie, so it uses local storage only.
- **Language and locale** come from the page address, such as `/locales/cy-gb/`. Changing the language moves you to that page in the other language. Nothing is stored.
- **Time zone** is shown by the picker and not stored.
- **Search** runs in the page. What you search for is in the address of the results page and is not stored by this site.
- **Share** opens the service you pick (email, LinkedIn or Bluesky) only when you select it. Nothing is sent before that.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking**, and no content from other sites is loaded.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes ([GitHub's documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages#data-collection)). That record is GitHub's, under GitHub's privacy statement. This project cannot see it.

## Removing what is stored

Choose the settings again, or clear this site's data in your browser's settings. Both keys are removed with it.
