# About

Project background, working practices and how to contribute.

The project aims to produce a patient record whose behaviour is specified, tested and documented, including what has not been done.

## Not for clinical use

Do not use this software to care for anyone, and do not enter real patient data. It has no clinical safety sign-off, no named Clinical Safety Officer, no clinical review and no regulatory approval of any kind. The project makes no claim to meet any regulation or standard.

## How the work is done

- **Spec first.** A behavioural change is one commit with a spec edit, a code edit and a test edit.
- **Stated limitations.** Every changelog entry states what was not done or not verified.
- **Clinical risk goes in the hazard log.** Only a Clinical Safety Officer may accept or close a hazard.
- **International design.** National rules are held in packs, not in the core.

Much of the work is done with AI coding agents. The [AI statement](https://github.com/electronic-patient-record/electronic-patient-record/blob/main/AI_STATEMENT.md) and the [agent instructions](https://github.com/electronic-patient-record/electronic-patient-record/blob/main/AGENTS.md) are public.

## How to contribute

The project has open needs for clinicians, a Clinical Safety Officer, and reviewers for its privacy and access work.

- Start with the [open questions](https://github.com/electronic-patient-record/electronic-patient-record/blob/main/spec/16-open-questions.md).
- Read [how to contribute](https://github.com/electronic-patient-record/electronic-patient-record/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Open an [issue](https://github.com/electronic-patient-record/electronic-patient-record/issues).
