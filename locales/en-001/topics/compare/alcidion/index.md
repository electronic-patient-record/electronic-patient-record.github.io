# Alcidion

An Australian-listed supplier of flow, observations and tasking.

A note from public sources, read on 4 November 2026. Nothing here was verified by using the product. Vendor claims are claims. This is not a product review, and "not found" means only that the sources read did not say.

## What it is

Alcidion Group is listed in Australia. Its main product is Miya Precision, which it describes as a FHIR-events platform, alongside Miya Observations and Assessments, the former Patientrack. Its markets are Australia, New Zealand and the UK.

## Care settings

Acute inpatient care and emergency flow are its evidenced strengths. Community nursing records and primary care were not established.
