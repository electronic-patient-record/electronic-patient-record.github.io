# Dedalus

A European group with hospital records, laboratory and imaging.

A note from public sources, read on 4 November 2026. Nothing here was verified by using the product. Vendor claims are claims. This is not a product review, and "not found" means only that the sources read did not say.

## What it is

Dedalus was founded in Florence in 1982 and says it has more than 7,700 staff in 40 countries. It bought DXC’s health business, completed in 2021. Products include ORBIS, ORBIS U and Lorenzo, which it announced in 2022 it would stop offering in the UK and Ireland.

## Care settings

Acute care is its core. A Care Manager module covers community-style care. Emergency and primary care were not found.

## Side by side

| Area | Dedalus | This project today |

## What this project takes from it

Silent non-delivery is the failure to design against, so extend the closed loop to letters. Products retire, so a record format must outlast its product, and a migration path should be planned from the start.
