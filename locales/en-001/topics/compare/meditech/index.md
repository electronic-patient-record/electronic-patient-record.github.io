# MEDITECH

A long-established US supplier, strong in community hospitals.

A note from public sources, read on 4 November 2026. Nothing here was verified by using the product. Vendor claims are claims. This is not a product review, and "not found" means only that the sources read did not say.

## What it is

MEDITECH was founded in 1969 and is privately held. Expanse, its web-based EHR, launched in 2018, and a hosted subscription is offered. Its main market is US community and mid-sized hospitals.

## Care settings

Acute and small or rural hospitals are its main footprint. The vendor also lists emergency, mental health, home care and ambulatory settings.
